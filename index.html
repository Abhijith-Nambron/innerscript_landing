The Tragedy of the Silent Archive

You write for years. In paper notebooks, notes apps, private documents, random chats, and scratch drafts.

That writing contains your emotional curves, recurring fears, repeated habits, relationship histories, buried ambitions, contradictions, and solutions to problems you solved years ago but have now forgotten.

But because notes are stored in linear lists or disconnected piles, 99% of your journals become a silent cemetery. You almost never go back to read them. You repeat the same mistakes, ask the same unresolved questions, and suffer from the same alignment loops because you cannot converse with your own long-range memory.

the_problem_statement.md
Your memory is scattered Across DayOne, Obsidian, paper, and Apple Notes
No simple way to connect ideas over time.
AI wrappers want your data They send text to public APIs.
They lack a localized privacy boundary.
They generate generic, shallow advice ("therapist-speak").
No source verification AI assertions about your patterns cannot be verified.
If AI says: "You tend to feel anxious in winter," it must link the exact December entries to prove it.

Two Loops of Self-Understanding

InnerScript is built on a tight feedback loop that keeps software simple, safe, and actionable.

LOOP_01

The Immediate Feedback Loop

Daily writing that returns active, grounded reflections without long-term setup.

  • Write safely: A localized, distraction-free markdown outliner.
  • Save reliably: Instantly keep entries in a local journal archive.
  • Immediate Reflection: As you write, receive exactly one grounded reflection question based *only* on the current stream.
LOOP_02

The Long-Term Memory Loop

Turning years of archives into a source-backed mirror of the self over decades.

  • Bulk Ingestion: Import years of DayOne, Obsidian, or Apple Notes archives.
  • Organized Memory: Turn older notes into a browsable history of recurring themes.
  • Ask and Cite: Ask your past in natural language and receive answers with precise sentence citations.
  • Pattern Detection: Discover emotional patterns, unresolved arguments, and habit triggers.
