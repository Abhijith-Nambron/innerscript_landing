The Tragedy of the Silent Archive

Your old writing already contains emotional curves, recurring fears, repeated habits, and relationship histories. The tragedy is not that you failed to write. The tragedy is that the writing stopped speaking back.

InnerScript brings old entries back as useful context when today's thought needs a longer memory.

Two Loops of Self-Understanding

InnerScript is built on a tight feedback loop that keeps software simple, safe, and actionable.

LOOP_01

The Immediate Feedback Loop

Daily writing that returns active, grounded reflections without long-term setup.

  • Write safely: A focused, distraction-free markdown outliner.
  • Save reliably: Instantly keep entries in a personal journal archive.
  • Immediate Reflection: As you write, receive exactly one grounded reflection question based *only* on the current stream.
LOOP_02

The Long-Term Memory Loop

Turning years of archives into a source-backed mirror of the self over decades.

  • Bulk Ingestion: Import years of DayOne, Obsidian, or Apple Notes archives.
  • Organized Memory: Turn older notes into a browsable history of recurring themes.
  • Ask and Cite: Ask your past in natural language and receive answers with precise sentence citations.
  • Pattern Detection: Discover emotional patterns, unresolved arguments, and habit triggers.

Building emotionally serious software.

We believe there is room for software that behaves like a quiet study. An archive that is completely owned by you, and designed to help you think rather than keep you scrolling.

InnerScript is our exploration of this idea. We are looking for collaborators who want to work on a personal memory system.
